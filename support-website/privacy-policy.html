Effective Date: February 12, 2026

App Name: TipSplit Calculator

This Privacy Policy describes how TipSplit Calculator ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information.

TipSplit Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data, usage data, or any other information from its users. The App operates entirely offline and on-device.

2. Data Storage

All data generated by the App, including calculation history, is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or any third party.

You can clear your calculation history at any time from within the App's History tab.

3. Third-Party Services

TipSplit Calculator does not use any third-party services, including but not limited to:

  • Analytics or tracking tools

4. Cookies and Tracking

The App does not use cookies, web beacons, pixels, or any other tracking technologies.

5. Internet Connection

TipSplit Calculator does not require an internet connection to function. The App works entirely offline. No data is ever transmitted over the internet.

6. Children's Privacy

Since TipSplit Calculator does not collect any personal data from any user, it is safe for users of all ages. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13 (or applicable age in your jurisdiction), because we do not collect information from anyone.

7. User Accounts

The App does not require or offer user accounts, registration, login, or any form of authentication. You can use TipSplit Calculator immediately after download with no setup required.

8. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade any user information with third parties because we do not collect any user information.

9. In-App Purchases

TipSplit Calculator is a one-time paid app ($0.99). There are no in-app purchases, subscriptions, or recurring charges. Payment is processed by Apple through the App Store, and we do not have access to your payment information.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Effective Date." We encourage you to review this page periodically.
