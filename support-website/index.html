About the App

TipSplit is a simple, paid utility app ($0.99 one-time purchase) designed to help groups split restaurant bills including tip and tax — quickly and accurately.

  • Enter your bill amount, choose a tip percentage, and set the number of people
  • See a clear breakdown: subtotal, tip, tax, total, and per-person amount
  • Quick tip buttons (15%, 18%, 20%) or use the custom slider
  • Split between 1–20 people
  • History of your last 10 calculations saved locally on your device
  • Full light & dark mode support
  • No internet connection required
